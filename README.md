# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior to understand purchasing patterns, customer preferences, and shopping trends. The goal is to extract meaningful insights from customer data to support better business decisions and improve customer satisfaction.

## Objectives
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns and shopping trends.
- Understand customer preferences and behavior.
- Identify popular product categories.
- Analyze customer segments and purchasing decisions.
- Use SQL queries to answer business-related questions.
- Generate actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python** – Data analysis
- **Pandas** – Data cleaning and manipulation
- **NumPy** – Numerical operations
- **SQL** – Data querying and analysis
- **Google Colab** – Development environment

## Project Workflow
1. **Data Loading:** Imported the customer shopping behavior dataset.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Checked and handled missing values, duplicates, and data inconsistencies.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Explored customer data to identify shopping patterns and trends.
4. **SQL Analysis:** Used SQL queries to answer important business questions.
5. **Insights Generation:** Analyzed the results to identify meaningful patterns in customer shopping behavior.

## Business Questions
- Which product categories are popular among customers?
- How do customer demographics relate to shopping behavior?
- How do discounts influence purchasing decisions?
- Which customer segments show different purchasing patterns?
- What factors may influence customer spending and purchase frequency?
- How can businesses use customer data to improve sales and customer retention?
